Scientific boundaries
---------------------
- UNIQUE is a metadata-recovery result.
- Structural *_GUESSED fallback is salvage, not a universal correction guarantee.
- Extended metadata damage never blocks reconstruction.
- Insertions, deletions, truncation and arbitrary framing loss remain unsupported.
- Audio/image reconstructed files are fresh canonical containers, not byte-identical copies of the original compressed input container.
